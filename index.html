Our Story

LAMÍ is inspired by the Cebuano word meaning delicious, tasty, or good flavor. Our restaurant celebrates Filipino cuisine by combining familiar flavors with a modern dining experience.


We believe that good food is more than just a meal. It is an experience meant to be shared, remembered, and enjoyed.
